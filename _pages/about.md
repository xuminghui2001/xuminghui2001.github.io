I am currently pursuing an M.S. degree in Mathematics at **Yanshan University** (since September 2024). Prior to that, I received my B.S. degree in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics from **Sichuan University of Science and Engineering** in June 2024.

My research interests lie in **nonconvex optimization**, **sparse representation learning**, **convolutional dictionary learning**, and **scientific computing / large-scale numerical linear algebra**, with applications in image restoration and 3D light-field microscopy localization.
